PEO health networks — every PEO, every carrier, every network, sorted National / Regional / Local

Each PEO's carriers come from its newest federal health filing (Form 5500 Schedule A, 2024 or later). The network is named by the PEO's or carrier's own plan documents (benefits guides, SBCs), saved in the evidence vault and checked against the carrier's monthly federal network file. Likely = named in a current document; Inferred = older, third-party, plan type only, or a two-step match. Tier: National = 40+ states, Regional = 3–39, Local = 1–2 (Blue plans count as Local in their home state and reach other states through BlueCard). A tier marked measured comes from the carrier's own network file; otherwise it is documented, and re-cite means the source page was general and needs a service-area page. Research repeats by itself 60 days before each PEO's renewal, and at once when a new filing lands.

Health data calendar — when each PEO's new data is due, and when it arrived

Checked every hour. Form 5500 (carriers and people covered) is due the last day of the 7th month after the plan year ends, or the 15th day of the 10th month with an extension (IRS); every DOL dataset file is under an hourly change check, so a new filing is picked up the hour DOL publishes it. SBCs must go out at least 30 days before an auto-renewing plan year (45 CFR 147.200), so network research runs 60 days before renewal. Carrier network files refresh monthly and are checked daily. Form M-1 (MEWAs) is due March 1 (DOL) — dated here, not yet loaded.
